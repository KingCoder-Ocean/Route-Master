Getting set up

    Plan a day

    Postcode run finder

    Type a postcode or a shop. The planner checks every day’s runs and tells you which run it fits best, how many extra miles and minutes it adds, and whether the driver still finishes inside the working day.

    Areas and days

    Which days a run passes each postcode district, from the schedule and from what was actually delivered. Use it to tell a customer when a van is already in their area.

    What the data shows

    What the planner has learned from your files, and the places where the schedule and real deliveries disagree.

    Vans and rules

    Vehicles

    Untick a vehicle that’s off the road. Put in each vehicle’s real mpg from the fuel card data; until then vans use the default van figure and anything over the truck payload uses the truck figure.

    Planning rules

    Match the planner to real journeys

    Plan a day, then take one run’s real miles and time from BigChange or Google Maps. Enter them here and every future plan is scaled to match UK roads.

    Your files

    Files are read on this computer only and never uploaded. Drop any of them in, in any order: the planner recognises each one by its columns.

    Make a ready-to-use copy

    Saves a copy of this planner with all the files you’ve loaded built in. Anyone who opens that copy, on any computer, can plan routes straight away without loading any files. Make a new copy whenever the schedule or addresses change.

    The copy contains customer names and addresses. Keep it on the company OneDrive or Teams, not in a public GitHub repository.
